Trust

You are holding other people's children's data. We treat it that way.

A school ERP holds minors' names, addresses, phone numbers, photographs, health notes and fee histories. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act that is the school's responsibility — so these controls are built in, not optional.

  • Data stays in India

    Every record — student, staff and financial — is stored in Indian data centres.

  • Role-based access

    A gatekeeper, a class teacher and a director each see only what their role permits. Nothing more.

  • OTP on sensitive actions

    Fee discounts and receipt cancellations can be locked behind an OTP, so no single person can quietly reverse money.

  • Full audit trail

    Every receipt records who processed it and when. Cancelled receipts stay in the ledger rather than disappearing.

  • Locks for closed periods

    Fee and exam periods can be locked, so last year's numbers cannot be edited after they are signed off.

  • Parents see one child

    The parent app is scoped to their own child's record. There is no directory of other families to browse.

Ask us something else

What is school ERP software?

School ERP software is a single system that runs a school's day-to-day administration — admissions, fees, attendance, exams, timetables, transport, library, staff records and parent communication. Instead of separate registers and spreadsheets, every module writes to one database, so a fee receipt, an attendance mark and a report card all draw on the same student record.

Does Edoovi ERP use AI?

Yes, in five places. AI generates the school timetable and resolves teacher clashes, face recognition marks student and staff attendance, transport predicts when each bus reaches its stop, reports can be asked for in plain English and projected forward, and the dashboard raises what has changed — a drop in collection or in a section's attendance — instead of waiting to be asked for it.

Can attendance be marked with face recognition?

Yes. Attendance can be marked by face for students and for staff, alongside the usual class-wise marking. It removes the roll call and closes off proxy attendance, and the result lands in the same attendance register — so cumulative percentages, low-attendance alerts to parents and the nine attendance reports all work exactly as before.

Can parents track the school bus live?

Yes. The parent app shows the bus on a map as it runs, along with a predicted arrival time for the child's stop, so nobody waits at the gate guessing. Inside the ERP it sits in the transport module on the Accelerator plan, and it can also be taken on its own if bus tracking is all your school needs.

How long does it take to set up Edoovi ERP in a school?

It depends on the size of the school and how much historical data needs to be brought across. Our team handles the setup with you and confirms a timeline after looking at your current records. Onboarding and training can be done on-site or remotely, whichever suits your staff.

Is there a free trial, and does it need a credit card?

Yes. Edoovi ERP is free for 30 days with every feature unlocked. No credit card is required, there is no setup fee, and nothing charges automatically when the trial ends.

Do parents get a mobile app?

Yes. The Edoovi Parent app is available on both Android and iOS. Parents see attendance, fee status and dues, homework, exam results, the school calendar, circulars and gallery updates, and can raise leave requests or concerns directly from the app.

Can we collect fees online through Edoovi?

Yes. Fees can be collected by UPI, net banking, card, cheque, challan or cash, and every mode posts back to the same ledger. Receipts are generated with a unique number and the due-fee report updates immediately.

Does Edoovi work for CBSE, ICSE and State Board schools?

Yes. The academic structure — classes, sections, subjects, exam patterns, grading and certificate formats — is configured per school, so the same system serves CBSE, ICSE and State Board institutions.

Where is our school's data stored?

All data is stored in Indian data centres. Access is role-based, so a class teacher, an accountant and a director each see only what their role permits, and sensitive actions like fee discounts or receipt cancellation can be locked behind OTP verification.

Does Edoovi ERP work without internet?

No — Edoovi ERP is a cloud system and needs an internet connection. That is what keeps every device, from the front office desktop to a parent's phone, looking at the same live data.
