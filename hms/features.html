Feature Breakdown

Clinical systems structured for performance & speed

Edoovi HMS integrates daily operations, digital documentation, and real-time medical intelligence. Use advanced AI agents to run error-free care workflows.

1. Core Administrative & Clinical Modules

Everything needed to run daily hospital workflows

OPD Management

Log consultations, patient vitals, and issue digital prescriptions (Rx) synced directly to the pharmacy. Complete history records are searchable by name or UHID.

IPD Wards Registry

Track bed occupancy, allocate nurses to specific wards, monitor inpatient vitals, and compile detailed admission logs.

Patient Registry

Biometric check-in, unified electronic health records (EHR), insurance verification logs, and patient portal setups.

Smart Billing

Consolidate pharmacy, lab tests, room charges, and diagnostic fees into a single invoice. Direct integration with major insurance carriers.

Lab & Pathology

Seamless sample tracking, direct connection with laboratory machines, and automatic PDF test report generation.

Medical Imaging Sync

View patient scans (MRI, X-ray, CT) directly in any web browser, eliminating the need for complex local viewing software.

2. AI Suite & Medical Intelligence Secure & Private

Automating prescription entries, patient reminders, and medical safety checks

WhatsApp Patient Assistant

Sends automatic appointment alerts and helpful messages via WhatsApp. Helps patients confirm their visits and provides timing updates.

Automated Discharge Summaries

Automatically pulls together a patient's treatment history, lab findings, and prescribed medicines to create a clear discharge report, ready for the doctor to review and sign.

Treatment Safety Check

Assists nursing staff during patient check-ups and medical procedures, cross-checking logs to help ensure everything is followed correctly.

Drug Safety Alerts

Automatically checks for potential conflicts or allergy warnings when a new medicine is prescribed, keeping patients safe and healthy.
