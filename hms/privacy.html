Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Healthcare Compliance (HIPAA & GDPR)

Edoovi HMS operates as a Business Associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect Protected Health Information (PHI) stored in patient registries and clinical summaries.

2. Data Encryption and Storage Security

All electronic health records (EHR), medical prescriptions, clinical billing logs, and diagnostic files are encrypted:

  • In Transit: Encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • At Rest: Encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) on AWS/Azure secured cloud server instances.

3. Information Collected & Processing

We collect system logs, medical vitals, diagnostic histories, and consultation summaries purely to run clinical intelligence scripts (such as generating discharge summaries and scheduling patient follow-up notifications). Edoovi never sells, shares, or utilizes patient record logs for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Session Management

We use secure cookies to maintain practitioner sessions, load hospital-specific configuration dashboards, and track operational metrics. Users can manage or revoke cookie settings inside their browser preferences at any time.
